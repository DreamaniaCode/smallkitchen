# Editorial library

30 US-English source-based articles, 18,190 body words plus FAQs; ten published and twenty drafts. Markdown copies are in content-library. Fifteen real licensed category photographs include attribution and caveats about model differences. The hero is labeled AI illustration. No invented test results, prices or ratings.

SEO title/description, canonical URL from APP_URL, Article and Breadcrumb structured data, sources, FAQ text, table of contents and related links are included. Drafts require the owner session to preview and are excluded from the sitemap. These checks are not search-volume evidence or ranking guarantees.

The final hosting target is Coolify with plain PostgreSQL and Prisma. Supabase is not required. See README.md.
