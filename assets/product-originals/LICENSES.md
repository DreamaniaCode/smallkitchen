# Real product-category photographs

All delivery assets were visually inspected. These are real photographs from Wikimedia Commons, not AI-generated retail product images. They are used as category references; captions must not imply a photograph depicts a different named model or our hands-on testing. No image was cropped or semantically altered.

The optimized WebP images retain the license listed for each source. In particular, CC BY-SA images and their resized/converted versions remain under the same share-alike license; this does not apply that license to unrelated site text or code. Display author, source link, license link and the change notice next to an image or on an accessible credits page. Original downloaded renditions are retained in `assets/product-originals/`. Some later downloads used the source’s 1200-pixel thumbnail rendition because full-resolution requests were rate limited.
